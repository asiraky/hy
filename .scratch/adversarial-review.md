   [ModelPicker.tsx:144](/Users/aaron/code/omniplex/.worktrees/feature-hy-bdc60144/web/src/components/ModelPicker.tsx:144) places a plain effort button inside the outer cmdk `Command`, but outside its `CommandList`. After pointer-opening the submenu, [EffortMenu.tsx:65](/Users/aaron/code/omniplex/.worktrees/feature-hy-bdc60144/web/src/components/EffortMenu.tsx:65) contains only non-tabbable cmdk items: desktop focus lands on the popover container and mobile focus lands on the sheet’s close button.    [ModelPicker.tsx:136](/Users/aaron/code/omniplex/.worktrees/feature-hy-bdc60144/web/src/components/ModelPicker.tsx:136) only includes effort in the trigger when the model currently advertises effort options. That directly misses the consolidation and trigger-label requirement for this reachable state.
